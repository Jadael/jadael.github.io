I organize information so people can find what they need. For five years I alone ran a 3,000-article knowledge base and help chatbot for 10,000–20,000 employees, using library-science methods: cataloging, controlled vocabulary, scheduled review and weeding. Earlier I worked in customer escalations, reporting and training, and ran a fine-art print studio. I'd like to bring that to a library, museum, or independent bookstore or game shop.

My goal is sustainable, healthy work towards universally accessible knowledge and artifacts that will still be of value to people living ten thousand years from now.
